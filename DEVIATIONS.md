# Deviations from NetHack

Every intentional rule difference from NetHack is logged here.

| Area                                   | NetHack behavior                                                                                                                                                                  | Our behavior                                                                                                                                                                                        | Reason                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RNG                                    | `rn2(x)` uses `RND() % x` (modulo bias)                                                                                                                                           | Rejection sampling, no modulo bias                                                                                                                                                                  | Uniform distribution; outcome sequences differ anyway (ref: src/rnd.c)                    |
| Level generation                       | Locked doors and trapped doors (and door mimics)                                                                                                                                  | Locked doors are closed doors; no trapped doors or mimics                                                                                                                                           | Kick/unlock/traps are not implemented in M1 (ref: src/mklev.c dosdoor)                    |
| Level generation                       | Boulders in blocked extra corridors                                                                                                                                               | No boulders; their rn2(50) roll is skipped                                                                                                                                                          | Objects are not implemented (ref: src/sp_lev.c dig_corridor)                              |
| Level generation                       | MAXNROFROOMS is 40; MAXRECT is COLNO*ROWNO/30                                                                                                                                     | Room cap is `max(40, floor(40 * w*h / (80*21)))`; the rect cap already scaled with area                                                                                                             | Large maps would otherwise stop at 40 rooms (ref: include/global.h, src/rect.c init_rect) |
| Level generation                       | dig_corridor gives up after 500 steps                                                                                                                                             | Cap is `max(500, 2*(w+h))`                                                                                                                                                                          | 500 is too short for forced joins on very large maps (ref: src/sp_lev.c dig_corridor)     |
| Level generation                       | Vaults, niches, themed rooms, subrooms, special rooms are made; stairs depend on dungeon level (no upstairs on level 1)                                                           | Only ordinary rooms and corridors; both staircases always exist (up in another room when possible, else a different cell of the same room)                                                          | Not needed for M1 (ref: src/mklev.c makelevel, generate_stairs)                           |
| Level generation                       | Rooms may end up disconnected (failed forced joins)                                                                                                                               | After makecorridors, any stray room is joined by a deterministic breadth-first corridor with plain doorways (no RNG)                                                                                | Guarantees the "all rooms connected" invariant (ref: src/mklev.c makecorridors)           |
| Level generation                       | Map is 79 usable columns (x = 0 unused), min size undefined                                                                                                                       | 0-based coordinates; internally x+1 is used so placement matches NetHack; minimum size 10x10 (smaller never places a room: create_room needs a 9-row free rectangle)                                | Any size must work; NetHack's rect.c limits make smaller maps impossible                  |
| Vision                                 | `clear_path()` Bresenham-style line-of-sight tables                                                                                                                               | Symmetric recursive shadowcasting; seen iff in LOS and (lit or adjacent)                                                                                                                            | Symmetric, artifact-free, fast on large maps (ref: src/vision.c vision_recalc())          |
| Vision                                 | Doors/walls at the end of a dark corridor are hidden unless an adjacent cell is lit                                                                                               | Not ported; lit-or-adjacent rule only                                                                                                                                                               | Simplification for M1; revisit with vision polish (ref: src/vision.c)                     |
| Doors                                  | Opening a door can fail ("This door is stuck") based on `rnl(20)` vs Str/Dex/Con                                                                                                  | Auto-open always succeeds                                                                                                                                                                           | Attributes don't exist yet (ref: src/lock.c doopen_indir())                               |
| Hero                                   | Starting inventory (+1 spear, dagger, +3 small shield, food ration) as real objects                                                                                               | Fixed stat block on the hero (`gear`: wielded +1 spear, AC 6); no inventory, dagger and food ration omitted                                                                                         | Items arrive in M3 (ref: src/u_init.c Valkyrie[], packages/content/src/hero.ts)           |
| Search                                 | `dosearch0()` uses `rnl(7 - fund)` with Luck, lenses/artifact bonus, and also finds hidden monsters and traps                                                                     | Secret doors and corridors only, with Luck 0 and no bonus; a found secret door always becomes a closed door                                                                                         | Luck, items, hidden monsters and traps do not exist yet (ref: src/detect.c dosearch0())   |
| Travel                                 | `findtravelpath()` falls back to guess mode (walks to the reachable cell nearest an unreachable target); `_` also offers `<` `>` shortcuts                                        | Unreachable/unknown targets are not offered and performing one costs no time; the path is re-planned every step over remembered map via `query.pathTo`                                              | Simplification for M2 (ref: src/hack.c findtravelpath(), packages/content/src/travel.ts)  |
| Travel                                 | `lookaround()` also stops for objects, fountains, etc.; `context.travel1` first-step quirks                                                                                       | Stops only for a visible non-peaceful monster adjacent, or a visible monster in the next path cell                                                                                                  | No objects/features yet (ref: src/hack.c lookaround())                                    |
| Melee combat: Luck, rings, encumbrance | Luck adjusts the to-hit roll, rings add to-hit/damage, burden and traps subtract from to-hit                                                                                      | None of these exist in M2; to-hit uses Str/Dex, level, AC, weapon enchantment and skill bonus only                                                                                                  | Systems arrive later (ref: src/uhitm.c find_roll_to_hit)                                  |
| Weapon skills                          | Skills train with use and add damage/to-hit by level                                                                                                                              | `gear.weapon.skillBonus` is a fixed to-hit value; the damage skill bonus is 0 (Basic); no training                                                                                                  | No skill system in M2 (ref: src/weapon.c weapon_hit_bonus, weapon_dam_bonus)              |
| Hero attack extras                     | Knockback on 1/6 of weapon hits, flee-on-low-HP chance, peaceful confirmation prompt, passive attacks, thick-skinned damage nullification, fleeing/stunned/trapped target bonuses | Not implemented; attacking a peaceful just makes it hostile, no prompt                                                                                                                              | Out of scope for M2 (ref: src/uhitm.c mhitm_knockback, attack_checks, known_hitum)        |
| Monster weapon attacks                 | AT_WEAP uses the wielded weapon's dmgval and hitval, or plain dice if unarmed                                                                                                     | Always plain `d(n,d)`; monsters carry no objects. Hug, engulf, gaze, spit, breath, explode and magic attacks are skipped; extra damage for undead at midnight and monster knockback are not applied | No objects or special damage types in M2 (ref: src/mhitu.c mattacku, hitmu)               |
| Kills                                  | A kill leaves a corpse and possibly death drops                                                                                                                                   | The monster just disappears; experience is still awarded                                                                                                                                            | No items until M3 (ref: src/mon.c xkilled)                                                |
